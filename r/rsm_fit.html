design_clean <- design[, !(names(design) %in% c("x1", "x2", "x3"))]

design_coded <- coded.data(design_clean,

Because peak_rate and time have no internal optimum (stationary points out of range), the optimum will be found on the border of the cube. In optim() we use method=‘L-BFGS-B’ with explicitly lower=c(-1,-1,-1) , upper=c(1,1,1) — otherwise the optimizer will suggest combinations outside the experimental range (like −85°C).
